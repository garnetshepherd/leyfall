LEYFALL

A free, offline auto RPG for Android.

Leyfall is an auto RPG with an action-RPG endgame. You build a hero, choose the modifiers for each map, and the hero fights on its own. The fight is shown as captioned stills and pauses when you have a decision to make.

It has no account, no ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases and no internet permission. You play it in portrait with one thumb. It draws a new picture every few seconds instead of sixty times a second, so almost all the battery it uses goes to the screen.

How it plays

You pick your hero's skills, link supports to them and set a trigger for each skill. You also choose how the hero moves in a fight: Kite, Hold Ground, Dive or Flank. Before each map you draft modifier cards. Each card makes the map harder and the rewards bigger, and matching themes gives set bonuses. During a map the game stops for decisions at rifts, ley shrines, sealed vaults, cursed chests, storm fronts, boss phases and near-death moments, and when you meet a rival salvager or your nemesis.

What's in it

The atlas has sixteen tiers of maps. Three guardians block the way deeper, three pinnacle bosses wait at the end, and after them you can keep pushing deeper with no limit. A nemesis moves around the atlas and gets stronger every time it escapes you. There are four classes, 24 skills, 40 supports and a 121-node passive tree. Orbs change an item's modifiers, and corruption can improve an item or ruin it. Some combinations of socketed runes add a bonus that you find by experimenting. You can trade at rival salvagers' stalls, and their stock gets better after you beat them on a map. League challenges, an atlas tree and color palettes for the interface give you more to unlock.

Join the closed test

Leyfall is in a closed test on Google Play, and anyone can join. Use the Google account that's on your Android phone. Join the leyfall-testers group, then open the testing page, tap "Become a tester" and install Leyfall from the Play Store link on that page.

Google needs 12 testers who stay in for 14 days before the game can be released, so please stay in the group and the test for two weeks. Feedback and bug reports go to the Leyfall Discord.
