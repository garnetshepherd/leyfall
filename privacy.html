Privacy policy · effective November 5, 2026

Leyfall does not collect, send or share any information about you. This policy covers the Leyfall game for Android on Google Play and this website.

What the game collects

Nothing. Leyfall has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It requests no Android permissions, so it cannot reach the network, your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

What stays on your phone

Your progress is saved in the game's private storage on your phone, where only Leyfall can read it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Android backup

If Android backup is on, your phone may include Leyfall's save in your Google account backup. Google makes and stores that backup under your account and Google's privacy policy, and the developer of Leyfall cannot access it. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

This website and the Discord server

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. If you join the Leyfall Discord server, Discord's privacy policy covers what you post there. If you email the developer, your email is used only to reply to you.

Children

Leyfall is not directed at children under 13. It collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
